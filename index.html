Why this matters

Ageism can show up in hiring, education, healthcare, media, and everyday relationships. It often goes unnoticed because it is treated as “normal” or “just a joke,” but it harms people and communities.

  • Limits opportunities for learning and work
  • Creates unnecessary stereotypes
  • Reduces dignity, trust, and belonging

What we believe

Everyone deserves to be seen for their capabilities, not judged by assumptions tied to age.
